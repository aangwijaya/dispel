Home · confidence states

Showing uncertainty honestly

Three Home states that follow the confidence guidance: show "unsure" as a real answer, keep certainty about a label separate from certainty about the outcome, and never present stale judgments as fresh. All values are mock data. The earlier design is on the left, the shipped one on the right.

1 · A clear pattern does not mean clear odds

The texture and the "confidence" word on a setup come from how sure the read is about the pattern label. The odds come from a separate yes/no judgment, and 53% is close to a coin flip. Today a clear pattern draws a solid bar and says "High confidence" next to near-even odds.

Odds certainty is derived from odds on the client, so older reads get it too. The detail keeps four wells (Odds, Pattern, Risk, Horizon); the texture carries the overall.

2 · Show the split behind "No clear read"

When stance confidence is below 0.5 the verdict falls back to No clear read, which is right. But the Confidence stat only shows "Low", so the user can't tell a quiet market from a market where two stances are almost tied. The stored probabilities already hold the answer: the top two stances and how close they are.

Rule. Low confidence with a top-to-second ratio under 1.5 reads "close split" and shows both stances. Otherwise it reads "spread out" and shows the top one. Data: optional stats.split (low-confidence reads) and stats.riskRaisedPct (risk raised above its most likely level).

3 · Say when a read is partial

status = 'degraded' reaches useMarketRead but nothing renders it, so a read built on carried-over judgments looks exactly like a fresh one. After this change a degraded read publishes no setups, so the empty list needs its own state. Otherwise it reads as "Nothing meets the bar", which would be wrong.

Prices refreshed at 14:47, but the market judgment carries over from 14:32. Setup odds depend on levels that have moved since then, so none are shown. Full read expected 15:02.

Rule. Same layout, like the C5 stale-read state: the caution chip, the verdict dims to Ash, and the setups list says "paused", not "nothing meets the bar". Data: status on the row drives the state; optional judgedTime on the payload gives the time.
