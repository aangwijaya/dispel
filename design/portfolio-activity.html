    Activity: safe by construction

    What changed from the MVP.

    AreaMVPNew
    FormOne row of selects and inputsA two-column form: asset tiles with monograms, network chips with the address format under them, an address field, then quantity with percentage chips.
    AddressFirst/last 4 in accent colourThe same rule in the new palette: first and last 4 in white 500, the middle in Smoke, Geist Mono. A valid address locks into a green-ringed view with a check. Invalid shows the expected format.
    Key guardWarning line onlyIf the field holds 12 or 24 words or a 64-character hex string, it turns into a rose danger block: “This looks like a seed phrase or private key.” The value is cleared and never stored.
    ReviewButton onlyA review strip spells out the move in one sentence plus cost basis, value and cash after. The button repeats the action: “Deposit 2.50 SOL ↵”.
    LedgerA flat tableGrouped by day with a day net. Direction is shown as a badge on the asset monogram. Filter by In/Out and Cash/Crypto. Addresses can be copied. New entries flash green once.

    Colour

    Semantic colour stays semantic.

    Deposits and withdrawals are not profit or loss, so the ledger never paints them green or red. Money in is Mist with a “+”, money out is Ash with a “−”, and direction is an arrow badge. Green and rose appear only where money was made or lost: P/L, the equity band and the closed-trade results.

    Allocation needs categories, so it uses a categorical palette taken from the artwork: periwinkle #8aa4ff, amber #ffc07a, lilac #c79bff, and hatched Ash for cash. It is used only for “which asset”, never for state.
