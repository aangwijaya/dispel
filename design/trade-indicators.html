Mockup · Trade chart · v0.1.5

Indicators on the trade chart

EMA 20/50/200 and SMA 20 drawn over the candles, RSI 14 in its own pane below. Synthetic BTC/USDT 15m data. The charts are pre-rendered, so they show in any viewer; with JavaScript on, the chips are clickable.

What changes in the toolbar

Two new timeframes, 1w and 1mo (Binance's 1M, labelled so it cannot be read as 1 minute), and one new indicator group (outlined). Setup levels and Volume stay as they are. With eight timeframes the bar takes two rows at 912px.
