The sigil on Trade

Three quiet places, never behind data.

Trade is where people act, so the sigil steps back. It appears in three places, each with a job:

Background rings. The Astrolabe's rings, ticks and nodes, with no arc and no line, sit very faint behind the top right and turn once every 300s. The panels are 92% opaque, so the rings only show in the gaps. This is the same layer as on Home, so moving between screens feels like one instrument.

The setup seal. When Trade is opened from a setup, the strip above the chart starts with a 22px seal: a ring, 12 ticks, and a coral arc whose length is the setup's odds. It says “this context came from the market read” without a word.

The spell's tip on the chart. The last price is drawn exactly like the tip of the arc on Sign-in and Home: a white core, a thin ring, and a coral glow that breathes every 4s. It is the only living point on the chart. Candles, levels and the crosshair stay plain.

No sigil sits behind the chart, the order book or the order ticket. Under reduced motion the rings and the tip stop.
