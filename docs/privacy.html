Privacy

Dispel stores a paper account, nothing more.

Dispel is a paper-trading demo. Prices come from public market data and every order is simulated, so there are no real funds, exchange accounts, wallets or keys anywhere in the product.

What we store

  • Account: your email address. With Google sign-in, also the name and email on that Google account.
  • Paper account: simulated cash, positions, orders and the transaction ledger, stored in Supabase.
  • Session: a refresh token in the app's local storage so you stay signed in on that device.
  • Crypto addresses you type in the transfer form: public strings kept only in your own ledger.

What we never ask for or store

  • Real funds, bank details, exchange API keys, wallet private keys or seed phrases.
  • Any signing capability: transfers are simulated paper movements.

Third parties

  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting for the paper account.
  • Google — only if you choose Google sign-in.
  • Binance, Gate, Hyperliquid, Coin Metrics, DefiLlama, mempool.space — public, read-only market and on-chain data. No account data is sent to them.

Retention and deletion

  • Market reads are public data, not tied to your account, and are kept for 14 days server-side.
  • Your paper account stays until you ask for deletion. Open an issue on GitHub or email the address below and it will be removed.
